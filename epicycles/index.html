What am I looking at?
Try this
  1. Watch the From Earth view. The planet drifts east (left), stops, loops back west, then turns east again.
  2. Drag Epicycle size to 0. Does retrograde motion still happen?
  3. Switch between Earth-centered and Sun-centered. Compare the sky track and the chart: they are the same. Both models predict the same sky.
  4. Pick Mars and watch when the badge turns red. Where is the Sun? (Look at "From the Sun".)
  5. Change the Epicycle period away from 1 year. Retrograde no longer lines up with the Sun. Ptolemy had to build that link in by hand.
  6. Turn on Numbered sight lines in the Sun-centered view to see Earth overtake Mars.
